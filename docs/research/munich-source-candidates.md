## Outreach questions (send to rights holder)

May we ingest and retain your event identifiers, titles, dates, venues, categories, and source links for commercial public discovery? May we display descriptions and images? What attribution, caching, refresh, deletion, rate limits, and downstream publication restrictions apply? Do you offer a feed/API and cancellation/rescheduling signals? Can you authorize a limited 30-day Munich coverage sample?

For each candidate record: legal entity/owner, source URL, terms URL and date, explicit permission vs assumption, auth required, observed upcoming Munich event count, distinct categories, field completeness, cancellation behavior, attribution placement, data retention and revocation. Do not mark a source as cleared on the basis of a public event page.
